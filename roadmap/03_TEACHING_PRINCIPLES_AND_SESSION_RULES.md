# 1. North-star pedagogik prinsip

**Teach for transfer, not for test completion.**

Har bir target quyidagi zinadan o‘tadi:

`See → Understand → Observe → Guided use → Controlled retrieval → Independent use → Real project → Delayed retrieval`

# 2. Visual-first usul

Visual faqat bezak emas. U:

- ma’noni ochishi;
- sentence architecture’ni ko‘rsatishi;
- vaqt munosabatini timeline’da berishi;
- process’ni flowchart bilan soddalashtirishi;
- collocation family’ni xaritalashi;
- real Classroom/CS kontekst yaratishi kerak.

Cold test yangi mavzuning boshlanishi emas. Istisnolar:

- diagnostic;
- oldin o‘rgatilgan material review’i;
- ungraded prediction;
- closed assessment.

# 3. One meaningful task at a time

Har turn:

1. bitta aniq task;
2. Sarvarning javobi;
3. content va language’ni alohida tekshirish;
4. priority correction;
5. kerak bo‘lsa natural model;
6. keyingi task.

Uzun lesson’ni birdan yuborish taqiqlanadi.

# 4. Correction protokoli

## Minor error

Misollar:

- bir harfli typo;
- accidental spelling slip;
- punctuation/capitalisation;
- meaning’ni buzmaydigan xato.

Qoidasi: **bir marta ko‘rsatish va davom etish**.

## Priority error

- current target;
- meaning-changing;
- confirmed recurring;
- important collocation;
- unnatural sentence structure;
- intelligibility’ga ta’sir qiladigan pronunciation;
- foundational gap.

Qoidasi:

1. birinchi urinishni ma’no buzilmasa interrupt qilmaslik;
2. `improved / recurring / new` deb ajratish;
3. sababni tushuntirish;
4. bir darsda maksimal **3 priority**;
5. bitta aniq model;
6. odatda **1 immediate retry**;
7. takrorlansa Error Bank/SRS;
8. ikkinchi marta ham chiqmasa modelni berib darsni davom ettirish.

# 5. Feedback tartibi

1. nima yaxshilandi;
2. qaysi eski error qaytdi;
3. qaysi new error paydo bo‘ldi;
4. ehtimoliy sabab;
5. top 1–3 priority;
6. bitta retry;
7. model va SRS;
8. next target.

Dalilsiz maqtov va kamsituvchi tanbeh yo‘q. Muhim xato qolsa `perfect` deyilmaydi.

# 6. 90 / 45 / 10 rejimlari

## Green 90

- 5 min — energy, mode, objective;
- 10 min — spiral recall;
- 15 min — explanation/input;
- 15 min — guided practice;
- 5 min — phone-free logical break;
- 20 min — active output;
- 12 min — project/transfer;
- 8 min — feedback/save.

## Yellow 45

- 5 min — check-in/recall;
- 10 min — highest-priority target;
- 15 min — speaking;
- 10 min — project component;
- 5 min — feedback/save.

## Red 10

- 2 min — state and target;
- 3 min — three SRS chunks;
- 3 min — one model + personal sentence;
- 2 min — short voice and save.

Red day — zero day emas. Ertasi kuni qarz yig‘ilmaydi.

# 7. Morning booster — 30 min

- 10 min — SRS + Error Bank;
- 10 min — listening yoki pronunciation;
- 10 min — one active output.

Ertalab katta yangi theory berilmaydi.

# 8. Non-negotiable session rules

- har darsda active output;
- maksimal 3 priority error;
- bir priority errorga maksimal 1 immediate retry;
- tanaffusda telefon yo‘q;
- 19:00 dan keyin new task yo‘q;
- missed work katta backlog’ka aylantirilmaydi;
- weekly review’siz keyingi hafta tasodifiy boshlanmaydi;
- site taskni ko‘rsatadi; pedagogik decision roadmap + ChatGPT’da;
- full corrected text’ni mexanik ko‘chirish yo‘q;
- yangi personal sentence va transfer task ishlatiladi.

# 9. Professional source policy

Reading/Listening uchun ustuvor manbalar:

- British Council LearnEnglish;
- Cambridge English;
- official IELTS;
- BBC Learning English;
- VOA Learning English;
- TED-Ed;
- Khan Academy;
- Harvard CS50;
- universitetlar;
- official technical documentation.

`Best` = mashhur emas, **daraja va objective’ga eng mos**.

## Reading metadata

- source;
- direct link mavjud bo‘lsa;
- estimated level;
- approximate length;
- objective.

## Listening metadata

- source;
- verified direct link;
- estimated level;
- duration;
- accent aniqlansa;
- transcript availability;
- current objective.

Sarvar materialni o‘zi qidirmaydi. Source detail, transcript yoki answer invent qilinmaydi.

# 10. Material design standard

Har professional module’da zaruratga ko‘ra:

- original meaningful illustration;
- visual grammar panel;
- vocabulary tiles;
- timeline/process/comparison/mind map;
- real classroom/CS scene;
- pronunciation panel;
- verified media link/QR;
- yetarli answer space;
- evidence-based answer key;
- native typeset text;
- accessible contrast;
- clickable contents/bookmarks.

Visual workload’ni yashirish uchun page count sun’iy kattalashtirilmaydi.
