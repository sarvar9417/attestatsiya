**Resolution:** MVP v1 uses newer scope. Older blueprint remains expansion reference.

## Scheduling conflict

Older: seven sessions, 11h45m module plan.

Newer: daily Green 90 / Yellow 45 / Red 10 + morning booster.

**Resolution:** 11–13 hours is weekly/module quality benchmark; daily actual operation adapts with 90/45/10 and module can span 10–14 days.

**Resolution:** specialist roles can still be used, but website becomes evidence/workspace layer. No need to preserve four chats as a hard technical dependency.
