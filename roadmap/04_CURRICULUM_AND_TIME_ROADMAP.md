# 4. Grammar and Sentence Engine — G1–G10

1. **G1 Sentence Core:** word order, `be`, `have`, pronouns, possessives, negative, `There is/are`.
2. **G2 Noun System:** articles, zero article, singular/plural, countability, quantifiers, demonstratives, possessive nouns.
3. **G3 Present and Questions:** Present Simple, `do/does`, `be` questions, Wh-, negative, frequency, imperatives.
4. **G4 Classroom Action and Modality:** `can`, `must`, `have to`, `need to`, `should`, polite `could`, `want/ask + person + to`.
5. **G5 Time System I:** Present Continuous, Past Simple/Continuous, main future forms.
6. **G6 Detail, Quantity and Comparison:** adjective/adverb, comparison, quantifiers, numbers, measurements, prepositions.
7. **G7 Verb Patterns and Clause Builder I:** infinitive/gerund patterns, object + infinitive, basic relative clauses, connectors.
8. **G8 Time System II and Passive:** Present Perfect/Past, Past Perfect, passive.
9. **G9 Logic, Conditionals and Reported Meaning:** zero/first/second; zarur bo‘lsa third; modal possibility/deduction, reported speech, logical connectors.
10. **G10 Academic and IELTS Control:** complex clauses, concession, hedging, academic passive, noun phrases, reference, parallelism, punctuation, editing.

# 5. 2-sentabrgacha 6 haftalik Classroom Activation

## Week 1 — Sentence Engine

- article, `be`, singular/plural, agreement;
- Data Representation sentences;
- final output: `bit`, `byte`, `nibble`, `binary digit`ni 60–90 soniyada tushuntirish.

## Week 2 — Definition and Instruction

- `X is...`
- `X is made up of...`
- `First..., then...`
- classroom instructions/questions;
- pronunciation: /ɪ/–/iː/, final consonants, `sixteen/sixty`;
- final output: 2-minute explanation + 3 instructions.

## Week 3 — Pilot checkpoint

- 3-minute microteaching;
- known questions;
- rescue phrases;
- method/load evidence asosida adjust qilinadi.

## Week 4 — Lesson segment

- 5-minute segment:
  - opening;
  - objective;
  - explanation;
  - example;
  - checking question;
- AI student questions.

## Week 5 — Q&A and real simulation

- 8–10 minute simulation;
- 3 unexpected questions;
- content va English separate assessment.

## Week 6 — Rehearsal and readiness gate

- 8-minute independent mini-lesson;
- 5 clear terms;
- 3 instructions;
- 2/3 understandable Q&A;
- rescue phrase bilan davom etish.

### 2-sentabr decision

- **Green:** 10–15 minute English segment.
- **Amber:** 2–5 minute prepared opening/explanation/instructions.
- **Foundation:** 30–60 second opening + one instruction.

Qaror 1-sentabr real audio/simulation evidence bilan qilinadi.
